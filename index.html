Eya

Eya is a private body signal journal for tracking hunger, cravings, meals, weight entries, and food photos. It is designed for reflection, not medical advice or dieting pressure.

Privacy Policy Support

Private by default

Your entries are stored locally on your device. Eya does not require an account.

Signal tracking

Separate hunger, cravings, and eating sessions so you can see patterns over time.

Safety first

Eya does not encourage prolonged hunger, restrictive eating, or medical decisions.
