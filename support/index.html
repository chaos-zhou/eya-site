Eya Body Signal Journal Support

Eya is a private body signal and meal rhythm journal for hunger, cravings, eating sessions, weight entries, and food photos.

Common questions

  • Where is my data stored? The current version stores records locally on your device.
  • Does Eya give medical advice? No. Eya is for personal reflection and is not medical, nutritional, or weight-loss advice.
  • Can I delete records? Yes. Open the records screen and delete individual entries.
  • Why does Eya ask for camera or photo access? Only to attach an image to an eating record when you choose to do so.
