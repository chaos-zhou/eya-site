Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Eya Body Signal Journal is a personal body signal and meal rhythm journal. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is stored.

Information handled by the app

Eya can store hunger sessions, craving sessions, eating sessions, manually adjusted records, weight entries, and photos you choose to attach to eating records.

Local storage

Eya stores app data locally on your device. The current version does not require an account and does not upload your journal entries, weight entries, or attached photos to a server operated by us.

Photos and camera

If you grant camera or photo library access, Eya uses that access only so you can attach a food photo or screenshot to an eating record. The selected image is stored locally with the app data.

Data collection

The current version of Eya does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking, or server-side account features.

Health and safety

Eya is not a medical device and does not provide medical, nutritional, or weight-loss advice. It is intended for personal reflection. If you have concerns about hunger, eating, weight, or health, please contact a qualified healthcare professional.

Deleting data

You can delete individual records in the app. You can also remove app data by deleting the app from your device.

Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, email chaosli920@gmail.com or use the support page: Eya Support.
